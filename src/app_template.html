Portal hemodynamic index for partial liver grafts

Computes the pressure index zP = (PVP − CVP)/5 and the flow index zF = graft PVF per 100 g ÷ donor PVF per 100 g, and reads the pooled risk of small-for-size syndrome from published living-donor series.

Research tool. This calculator does not estimate your patient's baseline risk. Across published series the reported outcome at the same gradient ranges from 0% to 17%, because the level depends on recipient severity, outcome definition and technique. What is transferable is the effect of changing the gradient, measured within each cohort: odds ratio __OR_ZP__ per unit of zP (95% CI __OR_LO__–__OR_HI__), i.e. __OR_MM__ per mmHg. It comes from centring each cohort on its own average (__NGROUPS__ groups, __NSTUDIES__ series, __EVENTS__ events / __PATIENTS__ recipients, weighted R² __R2__), and agrees with a stratified logistic fit (OR __OR_STRAT__) and a random-effects meta-analysis of the per-series slopes (OR __OR_META__, I² __I2__%). Not a validated individual predictor and not a medical device.
