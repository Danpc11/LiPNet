Portal hemodynamic index for partial liver grafts

Computes the pressure index zP = (PVP − CVP)/5 and the flow index zF = graft PVF per 100 g ÷ donor PVF per 100 g, and reads the pooled risk of small-for-size syndrome from published living-donor series.

Research tool. The risk curve is a pooled logistic fit to five published groups (__PATIENTS__ recipients, __EVENTS__ events; __STUDIES__), calibrated on group means; __ASSUMED__ of the __NGROUPS__ groups had no reported central venous pressure and 5 mmHg was assumed for them. It orders groups; it is not a validated individual predictor and must not replace clinical judgement or the ILTS-iLDLT-LTSI consensus thresholds (PVP <15 mmHg and/or PVF <250 mL/min/100 g).
