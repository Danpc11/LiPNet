Risk curve

The curve is anchored on the reference you chose; the markers are the current gradient and the gradient after the change. Without an anchor it shows the published groups, each centred on its own cohort.
What this does and does not give you. The effect of changing the gradient is transferable: OR __OR_ZP__ per unit of zP (95% CI __OR_LO__–__OR_HI__), __OR_MM__ per mmHg, from __NGROUPS__ groups in __NSTUDIES__ series. The level of risk is not: at the same gradient, published rates run from 0% to 17%. So the percentages shown are only as good as the baseline you supply. Research tool, not a medical device.
