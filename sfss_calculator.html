Risk curve

The curve is anchored on the reference you chose; the markers are the current gradient and the gradient after the change. Without an anchor it shows the published groups, each centred on its own cohort.
What this does and does not give you. The odds ratio is the within-cohort association of the primary analysis of the paper: OR 4.70 per unit of zP (95% credible interval 1.38–16.25), 1.36 per mmHg, from 7 groups in 3 series. It compares groups of one cohort; it is not the measured effect of lowering one recipient's pressure, and the groups were often defined by the pressure or by a manoeuvre chosen because of it. The level of risk does not transfer: at the same gradient, published rates run from 0% to 17%, so the percentages shown are only as good as the baseline you supply. Research tool, not a medical device.
